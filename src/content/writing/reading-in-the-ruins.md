---
title: Reading in the ruins
date: 2026-08-18
category: notes
description: On following the links that still lead somewhere.
tags: [Reading, The internet]
---
An old blog is a peculiar kind of ruin. The furniture is still in place. The dates have stopped. Half the links lead to pages that no longer exist, and the other half open passages into a conversation whose participants have moved on.

Reading it means giving up the expectation of freshness. There is no update to wait for, no current controversy to resolve. A sentence has to justify itself by something other than its proximity to the present.

I keep finding that this makes me a better reader.

The broken links are a reminder that preservation is work. Somewhere, someone kept paying for a domain. Someone left a directory alone. The web remembers only when people decide that it should.
