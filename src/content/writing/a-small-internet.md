**Status: ongoing.** This journal is the first part of the experiment.

What would a website look like if it did not need to grow? A place with enough structure to make things findable, and enough looseness to let them remain strange.

The working principles are simple:

- Every piece has an address of its own.
- The archive is a path through time.
- Reading does not require an account.
- The words should remain usable when the decoration is gone.

For now, the project is a practice of building and keeping such a place. Further notes will live here as the work takes shape.
