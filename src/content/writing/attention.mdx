The opposite of distraction is not productivity. It is attention.

We describe attention as something to budget, capture, invest, or spend. Even our attempts to recover it borrow the language of the forces that exhaust it.

What if attention is less like a currency and more like a relationship? Something that changes both the person looking and the thing being seen.

<hr />

An unfinished thought: to pay attention without expecting a return.
