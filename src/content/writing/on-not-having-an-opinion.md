title: On not having an opinion
date: 2026-08-07
category: fragments
description: Leaving a space between encountering something and deciding what it means.
tags: [Uncertainty]
---
There should be a name for the relief of realising you do not need to have an opinion yet.

Not indifference. Not the performance of neutrality. Just permission to remain with something you have not understood.

An opinion can be the beginning of thought. It can also be a way of bringing thought to an early end.
