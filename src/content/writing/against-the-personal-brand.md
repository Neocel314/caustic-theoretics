---
title: Against the personal brand
date: 2026-09-02
category: notes
description: A few objections to becoming a recognisable product.
tags: [Identity, The internet]
---
The instruction to build a personal brand assumes that the self is a problem of consistency. Decide what you represent. Repeat it. Make yourself easy to recognise at a glance.

But the most interesting people I know are difficult to summarise. Their concerns change. They contradict themselves. They follow an unlikely interest until it changes the arrangement of everything else.

There is a difference between having a voice and enforcing a style guide upon your life.

Perhaps a pseudonym can offer a little room for this. Not an escape from responsibility, but a loosening of the expectation that every sentence must reinforce a single, coherent identity.

I would rather make a place in which thinking can change than a platform on which it has to remain recognisable.
